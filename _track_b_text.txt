 ◆ Rank 01 · The winner 86 / 100 
 Drone-to-3D 
 SIH26158 · Stakeholder NTRO · Single-pass drone video → accurate 3D model 
 With ISRO’s DepthWizard set aside, this is the only remaining “jaw-drop” demo: hand the judges a drone clip, and minutes later they’re flying through a photorealistic, measurable 3D model of the scene. NTRO (national technical intelligence) is a top-prestige, deployment-hungry stakeholder, and the single-pass ask — reconstruct from one linear fly-over, not a slow multi-orbit capture — is a genuine research moat that filters out the 90% of teams who only know how to run YOLO. 
 Frontend React + react-three-fiber / Potree fly-through · capture → reconstruct → explore flow · on-model measurement overlays. 
 Backend FastAPI + Celery /Redis job queue orchestrating the pipeline · WebSocket progress streaming. 
 Engines / models COLMAP SfM → OpenMVS dense mesh, boosted by monocular depth priors ( Depth Anything V2 ) + learned MVS · 3D Gaussian Splatting render mode for photorealism. 
 Scale & storage Metric scale from drone telemetry / EXIF · object store for frames & models · PostGIS for geo-referencing. 
 ◆ Killer feature — the 10× differentiator 
 Single-pass, metric, and measurable. Two things rivals won’t match: (1) a learned depth-prior fusion that reconstructs cleanly from one low-overlap linear pass, and (2) click-two-points measurement with a real-world distance and an error bar validated against ground control. That turns “pretty 3D” into a surveying-grade tool.

 SIH26158 · NTRO · 86 / 100 
 Drone-to-3D wins. 
 With the satellite single-image→3D king removed, this is the sole survivor that maxes the two hardest-to-co-satisfy axes — a visceral live demo (20/20) and a real technical moat (18/20) — under an elite defence-intelligence stakeholder, with every dataset free today. The one knock, live-demo risk, is fully neutralised by pre-baking the reconstruction. 
 ◆ The pitch narrative — memorize this 
 “Give us one flat pass of a drone — no slow orbits, no ground-survey team — and we hand you a measurable 3D model of the site.”
 Then live: play a short drone clip → the pipeline reconstructs → fly the judges through the model → click two points to measure a real-world distance with an error bar → close on NTRO value: rapid recon, disaster-damage assessment, border & infrastructure mapping, instant digital twins. 
 The technical angle that guarantees marks: lead with the single-pass moat — low-overlap reconstruction fusing learned monocular-depth priors with classical MVS — then prove metric accuracy against LiDAR ground truth, and finish on Gaussian-splatting photorealism. Those three moves separate you from every “I ran photogrammetry once” team.
